hyprland-pets puts a little pet in a pull-down box at the edge of your Hyprland screen. Drag him out and he walks on your windows. Throw him, catch him, pet him, dress him up.

Small cow, lots going on

One small Python process that stays out of your way: clicks go straight through to your windows everywhere except the pet, and it hides while anything is fullscreen.

🪟

Walks on your windows

Lands on top of them, rides along when you move them, hops between them, and can even move into your terminal.

🎯

Throw and catch

Fling him off the screen edges and catch him on the way down. Wall bounces score extra, and your best streak is kept.

💗

Petting

Hold the scroll wheel and stroke him. Hearts float up, and keep going and he flops over, blissed out.

🎩

A whole wardrobe

16 accessories plus your own pictures, fitted by drag and drop. Seasonal ones to claim around the holidays.

🎨

Any colour cow

Recolour his skin, spots and lines on a colour wheel, or use any picture as your pet instead.

🌦️

Dresses for the weather

An umbrella hat in the rain, a scarf when it's cold, and rain or snow in his box. No account needed.

🎵

Dances to your music

Anything playing makes him bop, sway and headbang. With cava he finds the beat of the song.

🏍️

Goes for rides

Every so often he hops on a mini motorcycle, a car, a one-cow helicopter or a bike.

Lives in your Waybar

Install

One line, in a terminal inside Hyprland. It installs what's missing on Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, Fedora and Gentoo, adds itself to your Hyprland config and starts. It asks before using sudo or touching your config. Run it again to update; your settings are kept.

$curl -fsSL https://github.com/BugzySleeps/hyprland-pets/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash

Questions

Does it work outside Hyprland?

It's built for Hyprland. It draws with gtk-layer-shell, so it runs on other wlroots compositors and KDE too, but the window-aware parts (walking on windows, terminals) use Hyprland's IPC. GNOME and X11 aren't supported.

Is it heavy?

No: it's one small Python process, and it sleeps when nothing's moving. Clicks pass straight through to your windows everywhere except the pet, the tab and the box.

Does it send anything anywhere?

Only if you turn things on: the weather (Open-Meteo, just your town's coordinates) and the update check (asks GitHub for the latest version). No accounts, no tracking. Your "profile" is a file on your computer.

Can I use my own pet?

Yes: any picture works, PNGs with transparency look best. Larry gets the extra poses (sitting, sleeping, typing, riding) and recolouring, but every pet walks, gets thrown, wears accessories and dances.

How do I uninstall?

Run the installer with --uninstall: curl -fsSL …/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall. Your settings in ~/.config/larry stay unless you delete them.

How this was made: this whole thing was vibecoded with Claude Opus 5.5, just a fun little project because I was bored. Somehow it turned into a cow with a wardrobe, a weather app, a Waybar module and a helicopter. Enjoy Larry.
